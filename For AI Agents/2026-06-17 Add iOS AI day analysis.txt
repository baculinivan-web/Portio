Added the iOS AI day-analysis pull reveal.

The tracker screen now has a black AI analysis layer behind the main day surface. Pulling down on today's tracker reveals the analysis area, loads a short model-generated read on how the day affects the user's goal, and switches the bottom controls into one Liquid Glass prompt for asking questions about today's logged food.

The new AI path uses the existing OpenAI-compatible provider settings but stays separate from food recognition prompts and tool-calling, so nutrition logging behavior remains unchanged.
